Good morning, hockey fans. It's Monday, November ninth, and here's your rundown of Sunday night in the NHL. It was a small six game slate, with a last second winner in Winnipeg, a Cutter Gauthier hat trick in Anaheim, and a Hellebuyck shutout in Toronto. The Blackhawks were off. Let's go game by game.

In Winnipeg, the Jets beat the Lightning four to three. Winnipeg led three nothing in the third on goals from Cole Perfetti, Morgan Barron and Brayden Yager, who got his first NHL goal. Then Tampa came storming back, with Brandon Hagel and then Pontus Holmberg twice, the second one just twenty six seconds after the first, to tie it. Mark Scheifele won it on a five on three power play with one point six seconds left, a one timer after Perfetti stole the puck from Ryan McDonagh, and that was the game winner. Stuart Skinner made eighteen saves, Andrei Vasilevskiy made twenty one, and the Lightning lost Brayden Point in the first period to an undisclosed injury. Coach Jon Cooper said he might miss some time.

In Buffalo, the Golden Knights beat the Sabres four to one. Mitch Marner scored a short handed goal in the second, and Jack Eichel made it two nothing, which ended up being the game winner. Beck Malenstyn got one back for Buffalo, but Tomas Hertl scored twice in the third to put it away. Adin Hill made twenty seven saves, and Marner hit a hundred career points as a Golden Knight, the fastest in franchise history. Buffalo's Lindy Ruff blamed turnovers, including one that led to that Marner breakaway.

In Boston, the Bruins beat the Panthers four to one. Elias Lindholm opened the scoring on a goal that was overturned on review, then Michael Eyssimont and Morgan Geekie made it three nothing in the first, and Lindholm's goal was the game winner. Carter Verhaeghe got one back on the power play, and JJ Peterka made it four to one early in the third. Jeremy Swayman made forty saves, a season high, and Boston has won four straight. Florida's Paul Maurice said his team fell off after a good start, and the Panthers are now zero and four in division games.

In Toronto, the Devils beat the Maple Leafs four nothing. Timo Meier scored twice in the second period, and the second one, at twelve forty four, was the game winner. In Calgary, the Flames beat the Flyers four to three in overtime. Philadelphia led three to two on an Owen Tippett power play goal, but Joel Farabee tied it shorthanded on a breakaway in the third, and Matt Coronato won it on a three on one at four oh eight of overtime, which was the game winner. Devin Cooley made thirty two saves in his first start since coming back from injury. Dan Vladar made twenty two for Philly, and the Flyers have lost three straight. Tippett said they're playing a little bit scared, and the Flyers also got bad news, as Kevin Bahl is done for the season with a ruptured Achilles.

And in Anaheim, the Ducks beat the Penguins five to two. Mikael Granlund scored on the power play late in the first, and Cutter Gauthier scored twice in the second, the second one at nine eleven making it three nothing and standing as the game winner. Tristan Luneau added a power play goal, and Gauthier finished off the hat trick into an empty net. It's his third hat trick in five games, which ties the NHL record for hat tricks in a month. Laurent Brossoit made thirty two saves and is four and oh since being recalled, and Jackson LaCombe had three assists for a ten game assist streak. Kris Letang and Rickard Rakell scored for Pittsburgh with the goalie pulled, but the Penguins couldn't cash in on a long five on three in the second period.
