Good morning, hockey fans. It's Friday, November thirteenth, and here's your rundown of Thursday night in the NHL. Eleven games on the slate, two went to overtime or a shootout, and the Blackhawks finally ended their skid. Let's go game by game.

We'll start with the Blackhawks, who beat the Sabres three to two in overtime at the United Center. Buffalo scored first on an Owen Power goal, and Frank Nazar tied it on the power play before Tage Thompson put the Sabres back up on a power play of their own. Nazar tied it again in the third, tipping in a Sam Rinzel point shot, and Ryan Donato won it at three twelve of overtime off a drop pass from Anton Frondell. Spencer Knight made twenty one saves, and Alex Lyon stopped twenty two for Buffalo in his season debut. That snapped a six game losing streak for Chicago.

In Helsinki, the Hurricanes beat the Kraken four to one. Nikolaj Ehlers got Carolina on the board, and Sebastian Aho scored on the power play less than a minute later, which turned out to be the game winner. Jacob Valimaki added one in the second, Aho scored again in the third, and Freddy Gaudreau got Seattle's only goal. Carolina has won three in a row.

In Montreal, the Canadiens beat the Bruins five to two. Kirby Dach and Josh Anderson scored in the first, and Jake Evans made it three to nothing early in the second, and that one stood as the game winner. Cole Caufield and Phillip Danault piled on, and Casey Mittelstadt scored twice for Boston, but it was too little too late.

In New Jersey, the Devils beat the Capitals five to two. Evan Rodrigues scored on the power play, Ryan Leonard and Pierre Luc Dubois had Washington up two to one, and then Lenni Hameenaho tied it with a net front tip. Vladislav Kolyachonok made his season debut and scored the game winner early in the third, Stefan Noesen added a breakaway backhand, and Jack Hughes hit the empty net. Connor Hellebuyck made twenty six saves and is six and oh since landing in New Jersey, while Logan Thompson stopped twenty four. Washington coach Spencer Carbery said his team ended up cracking in the third.

In Tampa, the Lightning beat the Blue Jackets three to one. Brandon Hagel scored twice, and he's got five goals in his last three games. Jake Guentzel's goal in the third, in his seven hundredth NHL game, was the game winner. Dennis Hildeby made twenty eight saves, and Jet Greaves made twenty five for Columbus, who've lost three straight. Matthew Knies took the blame for a turnover that set up Guentzel's goal, and Sean Monahan left with an upper body injury.

In Ottawa, the Senators beat the Avalanche four to three in overtime. Shane Pinto scored twice, including the winner at two oh eight of overtime after he picked off a blind pass from Parker Kelly. Colorado led three to two with about four minutes left on a power play goal from Gabriel Landeskog, but Drake Batherson tied it, and Linus Ullmark made twenty six saves in his return from injury. The bigger story for Colorado is that Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar both left in the second period with lower body injuries.

In Toronto, the Wild beat the Maple Leafs five to two. Matt Boldy had a hat trick, and his third goal, early in the third, was the game winner. Kirill Kaprizov had three assists and a ten game point streak, and Quinn Hughes has a ten game streak too. Jesper Wallstedt made twenty six saves. Toronto has lost five straight, and Anthony Stolarz said the team keeps making big mistakes.

In Florida, the Panthers beat the Red Wings five to four. Detroit led four to one, then Florida scored four straight, and Anton Lundell finished it with the winner with two forty left, set up by a no look pass from Brady Tkachuk. Lundell had two goals, Carter Verhaeghe had three assists, and Lars Eller tied it in the third. Detroit has now lost five straight, and Todd McLellan said they were on their heels after a third period penalty.

In Winnipeg, the Jets crushed the Stars eight to three. Mark Scheifele had two goals and an assist, and his fourth goal of the night for the team, the one in the second period, was the game winner. He also tied Dale Hawerchuk for the most points in Jets and Thrashers history. Dawson Mercer scored twice for the second straight game, and Stuart Skinner made thirty saves. Jake Oettinger got pulled early in the third after allowing six goals on twenty seven shots, and Jamie Benn called it unacceptable.

In Utah, the Blues beat the Mammoth one to nothing in a shootout. Joel Hofer made twenty nine saves, Karel Vejmelka made twenty two for Utah, and Connor McMichael scored the shootout winner in the eighth round. Both teams went scoreless on the power play, and a St. Louis goal got wiped out on an offside challenge. Utah has lost four straight.

And on Long Island, the Islanders beat the Kings five to two. Matthew Schaefer scored on the power play and Victor Eklund's goal in the second was the game winner. Brayden Schenn and Simon Holmstrom scored too, and Bo Horvat hit the empty net. Quinton Byfield got a power play goal for Los Angeles, who've lost three in a row.
