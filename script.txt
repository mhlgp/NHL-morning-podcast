Good morning, hockey fans. It's Tuesday, November twenty fourth, and here's your rundown of Monday night in the NHL. It was a seven game slate with no overtime games that I could find, a hat trick in Winnipeg, a ten game Toronto skid finally coming to an end, and Kirill Kaprizov running his point streak to a franchise record. The Blackhawks were off. Let's go game by game.

We'll start in New Jersey, where the Devils beat the Blue Jackets three to two. Timo Meier and Brenden Dillon, with his first goal of the year, scored in the first period, and Charlie Coyle got one back on a breakaway. Connor Brown's snap shot in the second made it three to one, and that's your game winner. Mikael Pyyhtia scored short handed in his season debut after being recalled, but Connor Hellebuyck made nineteen saves and the Devils killed both penalties. Columbus also lost Mathieu Olivier late in the second with an upper body injury.

In Winnipeg, the Jets beat the Mammoth four to two, and it was the Gabriel Vilardi show. He scored twice in the first period, just over two minutes apart, and added an empty netter late for his second career hat trick. Morgan Barron's goal at fourteen minutes of the first made it three to two and was the game winner. Utah got goals from Andrew Peeke and Lawson Crouse and outshot Winnipeg sixteen to four in the second period, but Stuart Skinner made thirty four saves and Cossa couldn't get the Mammoth any help.

In Montreal, the Kings beat the Canadiens two to one. Alex Laferriere scored on the power play, Alex Newhook tied it for Montreal, and then Trevor Moore chased down a puck while two Montreal defensemen hesitated and beat Jakub Dobes for the winner. Darcy Kuemper made twenty nine saves. Dylan Larkin had an assist in his Canadiens debut, and Drew Doughty played his thirteen hundredth game. Montreal had a Zuccarello goal waved off for goalie interference and pulled Dobes late, but couldn't tie it.

In Ottawa, the Senators beat the Flames two to one. Michael Amadio and Shane Pinto scored in the first period, and Pinto's goal was the game winner. Matt Coronato got one back on the power play in the third. Linus Ullmark made thirty five saves and is now twelve and one on the year. Ottawa's point streak is at nine games. Calgary has lost three straight and lost Morgan Frost to an upper body injury after a hit from Tyler Kleven.

In New York, the Maple Leafs beat the Islanders four to two, and Toronto finally snapped a nine game losing streak. The Isles led two nothing on goals from Anthony Duclair and Mathew Barzal, who scored his first of the season. Then Toronto outshot them twenty six to seven over the last two periods. Dakota Joshua cut it to two to one, Chris Tanev tied it in the third, and Gavin McKenna scored the go ahead goal, which is the game winner. Cowan added an empty netter. Anthony Stolarz made twenty saves. Auston Matthews also stretched his road point streak to start the season to ten games, a Leafs record. The Islanders lost Kyle Palmieri to a lower body injury.

In Seattle, the Kraken beat the Ducks three to two, and everything happened in the third period. Ben Meyers scored first, Cutter Gauthier tied it, and Vince Dunn put Seattle back ahead. Sam Colangelo tied it again, and then Kaapo Kakko deked Lukas Dostal for the winner with under three minutes left. Joey Daccord made the key stops, including one on Troy Terry late. Gauthier now has eighteen goals this month, two short of the NHL record for one month. Anaheim has lost four straight.

And in San Jose, the Wild beat the Sharks five to two. Joel Eriksson Ek scored twice, and his second, thirteen seconds into the second period, was the game winner. Kaprizov scored on the power play and added three assists, which stretches his point streak to fifteen games, the longest in Wild history. Matt Boldy had three assists, Faber scored, and Blake Coleman hit the empty net. Filip Gustavsson made twenty two saves in his season debut after offseason hip surgery. Tyler Toffoli and Alexander Wennberg scored for San Jose.
