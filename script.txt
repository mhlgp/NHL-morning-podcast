Good morning, hockey fans. It's Saturday, November fourteenth, and here's your rundown of Friday night in the NHL. It was a quiet one, just two games on the slate, and one of them went to a shootout. Let's go through them.

In Vancouver, the Rangers beat the Canucks five to one. Elias Pettersson scored first for Vancouver, but Gabe Perreault tied it late in the first, and then New York blew it open with four goals in the second period. Oliver Bjorkstrand got one on the power play, Alexis Lafreniere scored, and Glenn Gawdin scored twice, including the game winner at thirteen minutes of the second. Those were the first two NHL goals of his career, in his fifteenth game, and the second one deflected in off his skate. Lafreniere and Perreault each had a goal and an assist, J.T. Miller had two assists, and Dylan Garand made twenty seven saves. Leevi Merilainen made twenty five saves for Vancouver but got hung out to dry by a second period full of turnovers, and coach Manny Malhotra called it turnover after turnover after turnover. The Canucks have now lost seven straight. One bright spot, Brock Boeser had an assist and hit five hundred career points.

In Las Vegas, the Predators beat the Golden Knights three to two in a shootout. Ryan O'Reilly scored first for Nashville, Victor Olofsson tied it, and Rasmus Andersson put Vegas up in the second on a four on four goal. O'Reilly tied it again in the third with a shot from the right circle. Filip Forsberg and Roman Josi both scored in the shootout, and Josi's goal was the winner. Juuse Saros was the star, making forty saves, while Adin Hill stopped thirty for Vegas. Vegas coach Ryan Craig said his team didn't manage pucks well in the third, and Andersson said the giveaways wore them down. Nashville has won seven in a row, tying Carolina for the longest streak in the league, and Josi now has twenty one assists through twenty games, a first for a Predators player. Mitch Marner extended his home point streak to ten games.
