Good morning, hockey fans. It's Sunday, November eighth, and here's your rundown of Saturday night in the NHL. It was a big twelve game slate, with two overtime finishes, a Hurricanes blowout of the Blackhawks, a Devils comeback, and Sidney Crosby climbing the all time points list. Let's go game by game.

In Washington, the Capitals beat the Panthers four to three. Jack St. Ivany opened the scoring with his first NHL goal, and Brady Tkachuk scored twice for Florida, but Alex Tuch tied it in the second and Aliaksei Protas put Washington up three to two, which was the game winner. Pierre-Luc Dubois added another goal, and Logan Thompson made twenty seven saves, while Akira Schmid made twenty seven for Florida. Matthew Tkachuk had three assists and Sam Bennett scored late, but Florida's coach Paul Maurice said they couldn't handle the turnovers and odd man rushes. Tom Wilson got a boarding major and a game misconduct, and Sandis Vilmanis left hurt and didn't come back.

In Nashville, the Predators beat the Blues three to one. Jimmy Snuggerud scored on a first period power play for St. Louis, then Steven Stamkos tied it on the power play for his two hundred forty eighth career power play goal, which puts him fifth all time. Ross Colton's goal in the second was the game winner, and Nick Perbix hit the empty netter. Juuse Saros made twenty five saves, and the Blues, who've lost three straight, managed just six shots in the second period.

In Seattle, the Rangers beat the Kraken four to three. New York scored three times in the first period, Gabe Perreault, Tye Kartye and Jaroslav Chmelar, and Pavel Dorofeyev made it four nothing in the second for the game winner. Seattle goalie Joey Daccord was pulled after three goals on ten shots, and Philipp Grubauer stopped fifteen in relief. The Kraken rallied with three in the third from Kaapo Kakko, Jordan Eberle and Vince Dunn, but it wasn't enough. Joonas Korpisalo made twenty five saves in his first game back from injury, and Rangers defenseman Adam Fox became the fastest Rangers blueliner to reach twenty assists in a season.

In Toronto, the Canadiens beat the Maple Leafs four to two. Alex Newhook scored twice in the first period, and Chris Kreider's goal in the second made it three nothing and was the game winner. Toronto climbed back within one in the third on goals from William Nylander and Gavin McKenna, but Zachary Bolduc scored on the power play to seal it. Rookie goalie Jacob Fowler made twenty six saves and is eight, one and one, and Montreal's point streak is up to ten games. McKenna said two of his turnovers led to goals, and Toronto lost Morgan Rielly to a lower body injury.

On Long Island, the Devils beat the Islanders five to four in overtime. Jack Hughes ended it on a breakaway at one eighteen of overtime, set up by his brother Luke, and the Devils came back from a two goal deficit in the third. Dougie Hamilton tied it on the power play, and Luke Evangelista, Evan Rodrigues and Declan Chisholm also scored. Ilya Sorokin stopped forty five shots for New York, and coach Pete DeBoer called it his best game of the year, but the Islanders have now lost three straight.

In Ottawa, the Senators beat the Mammoth four to two. Fabian Zetterlund scored on the power play and Michael Amadio gave Ottawa the lead in the third, but Barrett Hayton tied it, and then Nick Cousins redirected a Thomas Chabot pass for the game winner with just over two minutes left. Shane Pinto hit the empty netter in his three hundredth NHL game, and Samuel Ersson made thirty saves for his third straight win. Clayton Keller had a goal and an assist for Utah, who've lost two straight.

In Minnesota, the Lightning beat the Wild four to three in overtime. Minnesota jumped out to a two nothing lead early and was up three to one in the third, but Jake Guentzel scored twice for Tampa, and Charle-Edouard D'Astous tied it with a six on four power play goal. Nikita Kucherov won it on the power play at three twenty three of overtime. Brayden Point had two assists to reach seven hundred career points, and Dennis Hildeby made nineteen saves, while Calvin Pickard made thirty for Minnesota. Scott Sabourin and Marcus Foligno also dropped the gloves.

In Dallas, the Stars beat the Sharks four to three. Thomas Harley had a goal and two assists, all on the power play, and his goal made it four to two and was the game winner. Dallas got three power play goals in all, and Jake Oettinger made twenty saves. Will Smith scored a five on three power play goal late for San Jose, but they've now lost five straight, and Macklin Celebrini had three assists in twenty eight minutes of ice time. Jamie Benn also picked up his nine hundred ninety ninth career point.

In Raleigh, the Hurricanes beat the Blackhawks nine to two. Jordan Martinook scored twice in the first period, and his first one, at ten fifty four, was the game winner. Seth Jarvis, Jackson Blake, Andrei Svechnikov twice, Sebastian Aho, Alexander Nikishin and Shayne Gostisbehere also scored. More on the Hawks in a bit.

In Denver, the Avalanche beat the Red Wings four nothing. All four Colorado goals came on the power play. Martin Necas scored twice, and his first, in the second period, was the game winner. Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar also scored, and Mackenzie Blackwood made twenty seven saves for his first shutout of the year. Detroit took five penalties in a ten minute stretch, and coach Todd McLellan said that changed the game. It's their third straight loss.

In Los Angeles, the Penguins beat the Kings five to two. Tommy Novak scored twice in the first six minutes, and Egor Chinakhov's power play goal late in the first made it three to one and was the game winner. Sidney Crosby scored twice, once into an empty net, and passed Marcel Dionne for sixth on the NHL's all time points list. Evgeni Malkin had three assists and moved past Adam Oates. Sergei Murashov made twenty six saves for his first win of the season, ending a long road losing streak. Darcy Kuemper made fourteen saves for Los Angeles, and Artemi Panarin had a goal overturned on an offside challenge.

And in Edmonton, the Oilers beat the Canucks four to two. Connor McDavid had three assists and has a point in all eighteen games this season. Devon Levi made twenty saves in his return from injury, and Leevi Merilainen made twenty six for Vancouver, who lost their fifth straight and are three, fifteen and one.
