Good morning, hockey fans. It's Friday, November sixth, and here's your rundown of Thursday night in the NHL. It was a big eleven game slate, with a Cooley hat trick in Montreal, four overtime or shootout finishes, a wild rally in Anaheim, and another loss for the Blackhawks in Tampa. Let's go game by game.

In Tampa, the Lightning beat the Blackhawks four to one. Victor Hedman opened the scoring on the power play, Ilya Mikheyev, who used to play for Chicago, scored twice, and Ryan McDonagh's goal early in the third made it three nothing and was the game winner. Andrei Vasilevskiy made twenty five saves and was one minute from a shutout, until rookie Roman Kantserov scored with fifty seconds left. Arvid Soderblom made thirty one saves for Chicago, but a couple of the goals were ones he'd like back. It was Tampa's eighth straight win at home to start the year. More on the Hawks in a bit.

In Montreal, the Canadiens beat the Mammoth five to four in overtime. Utah's Logan Cooley had a hat trick, including two power play goals, but Montreal kept coming back, with Nick Suzuki scoring in his return from injury, and Kirby Dach and Adam Engstrom, who got his first NHL goal, tying it at three and then four in the third. Cole Caufield won it thirty seconds into overtime after faking a slap shot on a two on one, and that's the game winner. Jakub Dobes made sixteen saves for Montreal, and Sebastian Cossa made thirty for Utah.

In New Jersey, the Devils beat the Blues five to four in overtime. The Devils led four to one after two periods, and Mantha scored twice, but St. Louis scored three goals in about three minutes in the third to tie it, with McTavish, Broberg and Dvorsky all scoring. Jesper Bratt ended it on a breakaway after a Dougie Hamilton pass, one minute and twenty six seconds into overtime, and that was the game winner. That's three straight wins for the Devils, and a tough one for the Blues to give away.

In Carolina's visit to Long Island, the Hurricanes beat the Islanders five to three. Stankoven and Kotkaniemi scored in the first four minutes, New York answered with three goals in the second, and then William Carrier backhanded a rebound at the edge of the crease late in the third for the game winner. Jalen Chatfield added an empty netter, and Brandon Bussi made twenty four saves, including two breakaway stops. Sebastian Aho passed Eric Staal for second in franchise history with his one hundred eighty seventh multipoint game, and Taylor Hall returned from injury. Semyon Varlamov made thirty four saves in the loss, and the Islanders went zero for four on the power play.

In New York, the Rangers beat the Flyers three to two. Philadelphia took a two to one lead in the second period, but Pavel Dorofeyev tied it in the third and then Tyler Kartye scored about ninety seconds later for the game winner. Adam Fox had two assists for the Rangers, who got their second straight win. The Flyers have lost two in a row and sit near the bottom of the standings.

In Pittsburgh, the Bruins beat the Penguins six to two. Pavel Zacha scored twice, Elias Lindholm's goal late in the first made it three to one and was the game winner, and Morgan Geekie scored with an assist. Michael DiPietro made sixteen saves in the win, and Boston has now won three in a row. The Penguins have lost four straight. Evgeni Malkin came back from injury, but rookie James Hagens left early in the third after a cross check from Samuel Girard.

In Detroit, the Golden Knights beat the Red Wings two to one. Detroit's Johansson scored early in the third, Braeden Bowman tied it for Vegas two minutes later, and Trevor Connelly won it with one minute and forty two seconds left. Carter Hart made twenty five saves for Vegas, and Victor Olofsson was back in the lineup after being reinstated.

In Columbus, the Blue Jackets beat the Stars two to one in a shootout. Mikko Rantanen put Dallas ahead in the third, Zach Werenski tied it with just over three minutes left, and Valeri Nichushkin and Kent Johnson scored in the shootout. Columbus is now five and oh in games that go past regulation, and Dallas was without Miro Heiskanen.

In Winnipeg, the Jets crushed the Canucks six to two. Dawson Mercer scored his first goal as a Jet, and his second assist and goal gave Winnipeg a three to one lead in the first, which stood as the game winner. Scheifele, Pionk, Ferraro, Iafallo and Koepke also scored, and Stuart Skinner made twenty four saves. Vancouver has now lost four in a row.

In Edmonton, the Ducks beat the Oilers five to four. Anaheim jumped out to a five to two lead, with Mintyukov scoring twice and Sennecke twice, including the game winner early in the third, and Cutter Gauthier had a goal and an assist. Edmonton made a late push on goals from Kapanen and then Draisaitl with forty five seconds left, and Connor McDavid had a goal and three assists, but it wasn't enough. That's three straight wins for Anaheim.

And in Colorado, the Predators beat the Avalanche three to two in overtime. Steven Stamkos won it on the power play two minutes and eighteen seconds into overtime, with a one timer off a Roman Josi pass, his two hundred forty seventh career power play goal, which ties Luc Robitaille for fifth all time. Justus Annunen made thirty saves, and Vitali Pinchuk scored his first NHL goal. The Avalanche have lost two straight.

Now a few league headlines. First, the confirmed news. Pro Hockey Rumors reports the Rangers put Sean Durzi on injured reserve and waived James Reimer. The Blue Jackets' Emil Andrae and Danton Heinen cleared waivers, the Islanders signed Ben Hutton to a one year deal, and the Kraken recalled Jagger Firkus. Detroit says Marco Kasper and Mason Appleton should be back during the team's road trip, and Flyers defenseman Rasmus Ristolainen skated but isn't ready to return. The trade deadline is Monday, March first, which is one hundred fifteen days away.

On the rumor side, all of this is rumor and not confirmed. Frank Seravalli reports Tampa is telling teams it won't move Nikita Kucherov, and there are reports that a lot of teams have at least talked about it internally. There's also a report that Detroit wants to explore more teams for Dylan Larkin, but his camp has declined to expand his approved trade list.

Now the Blackhawks, who lost four to one in Tampa on Thursday night and have now dropped five straight. They're four, twelve and one, and one and seven and one over their last nine. A Frank Nazar goal in the second period was overturned on a video review for a high stick, and Dillon Boucher fought. The bright spot was Kantserov, whose goal with fifty seconds left stretched his point streak to seven games, five goals and two assists, tying the sixth longest rookie streak in Blackhawks history. Sam Rinzel and Ethan Del Mastro had the assists. Zemgus Girgensons, a Lightning player, came back from injury for Tampa, and Brayden Point's point streak against Chicago is up to twelve games. Coach Blashill praised Mikheyev as a momentum changer and said his team needs to get to the net more, and said they'll win or lose as a team when asked about Soderblom, who is winless in four starts this year.

The feel around the team is that the Bedard and Kantserov pairing is the one real bright spot, while the goaltending and defense keep giving games away. Writers and fans keep pointing at Soderblom's soft goals and wondering if the backup spot needs a different answer, and they say the team gives up too many chances against. Plenty of people also think the youth at center is showing, and Blashill talking about internal analytics after losses has drawn some eye rolls, even though Bedard has come out in defense of his coach.

On the roster, Patrick Kane left Monday's game with an upper body injury, and Blashill doesn't expect him to play Saturday in Carolina. Bowen Byram is on injured reserve, and Dillon Boucher was recalled from Rockford.

Chicago plays next on Saturday at Carolina, with the NHL schedule showing it's a seven o'clock start Central. They're seven, two and one over their last ten and have won five of their last six, including Thursday's win on Long Island. Logan Stankoven has eleven goals, Jackson Blake has an eight game point streak, Sebastian Aho has twenty three points, and Taylor Hall is back in the lineup. After that, the Hawks are home Thursday against Buffalo, and then at Winnipeg on Saturday the fourteenth.
